what this says
==========================================================================
  dropping the behavioral columns costs +0.1651 recall on the same population.
  the remaining gap to the old joined-population run is +0.0237 recall.
  The behavioral columns account for most of the change: RF's zero-day recall came
  from the features, so Option A's conclusion that behavioral features could not
  help RF was wrong, and the fusion rule's lost margin is a real consequence of
  finally giving RF those columns.
  Either way this is one ablation on one dataset and does not establish which fusion
  rule to deploy; it establishes what caused the number to move.
